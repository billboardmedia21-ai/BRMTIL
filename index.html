Event Summary.

Reimagining the future of health through breakthroughs in regenerative medicine and longevity science. Join global leaders, researchers, and innovators shaping the next era of human lifespan and vitality. Discover ideas, technologies, and conversations that are redefining how we heal, age, and thrive.

Non-communicable diseases such as Diabetes, Hypertension, High Cholesterol, Heart Diseases, and Cancers are rapidly increasing across India. Along with this rising prevalence, the complications associated with these conditions are also becoming more serious and challenging.

Despite advances in current treatment protocols and medical methodologies, long-term disease control and prevention of complications still remain a major concern.

To address these challenges, the integration of Regenerative Medicine and Individualized Longevity Strategies into regular clinical practice is emerging as a revolutionary approach in modern healthcare. These innovative strategies have the potential to improve disease outcomes, reduce complications, and enhance the overall quality of life of patients.

We are privileged to welcome one of the world's most renowned Bio-Regenerative Medicine Scientists, Prof. Dr. Mike Chan, along with his expert team, who will be sharing their invaluable knowledge, clinical expertise, and latest advancements in the field.

This Conference & Workshop will provide a unique opportunity for healthcare professionals to gain practical insights into the latest developments, evidence-based approaches, and real-world clinical applications in Regenerative Medicine and Longevity.

Join us in shaping the future of healthcare and longevity medicine.
